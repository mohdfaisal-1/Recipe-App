# 🍲 Recipe App

A modern and user-friendly application designed to help food enthusiasts discover, save, and explore a wide variety of delicious recipes with ease.

---

## 🚀 Features

- **Recipe Discovery:** Browse through a diverse collection of recipes.
- **Detailed Instructions:** View step-by-step cooking directions, ingredient lists, and prep times.
- **Search & Filter:** Quickly find recipes based on your cravings, categories, or available ingredients.
- **Responsive UI:** Built with a clean, intuitive, and mobile-friendly user interface.
